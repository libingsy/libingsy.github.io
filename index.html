Bing Li

Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

Email: bing.li@ntu.edu.sg

Dr. Bing Li received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Applications Technology from Beihang University in 2024, where he studied at the School of Computer Science and was co-advised by Professor Di Huang and Assistant Professor Jiaxin Chen. From October 2024 to October 2025, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR), A*STAR, Singapore, under the supervision of Professor Qing Guo. Since January 2026, he has been a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, working with Professor Wee Peng Tay on UAV perception systems.

Research Interests. UAV perception, multi-modal 3D perception, autonomous driving safety, image/video understanding, 3D generation, and adversarial attacks.
