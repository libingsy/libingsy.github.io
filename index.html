Bing Li

Postdoc Researcher at A*STAR (Agency for Science, Technology and Research), Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR), Institute of High Performance Computing (IHPC), Singapore.

Email: libingsy@buaa.edu.cn, libingsy@hotmail.com

Dr. Bing Li received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Applications Technology from Beihang University in 2024, where he studied at the School of Computer Science and was co-advised by Professor Di Huang and Assistant Professor Jiaxin Chen. From 2024 to 2025, he worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at A*STAR, Singapore, under the supervision of Dr. Qing Guo.

Research Interests. Image/video analysis, representation learning, and 3D adversarial attacks.
